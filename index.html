HOW A COORDINATION CYCLE RUNS

One federated model, every discipline accountable to it.

Each discipline models to the agreed standard, the federated model is checked against every other layer, and issues are logged, assigned, and closed before the next cycle opens.

  • Architectural model — spatial envelope and finishes
  • Structural model — framing, foundations, load paths
  • MEP model — ductwork, piping, conduit routing
